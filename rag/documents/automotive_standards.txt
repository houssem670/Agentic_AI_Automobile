Title: Automotive Cybersecurity and Functional Safety Standards
Domain: Automotive Engineering
Purpose: Provide context for security and safety investigations

1. ISO/SAE 21434
ISO/SAE 21434 addresses cybersecurity engineering for road vehicles.
It covers cybersecurity activities across the vehicle lifecycle, including
risk assessment, development, production, operation and maintenance.
It provides an engineering framework rather than a universal CAN attack
detection algorithm.

2. ISO 26262
ISO 26262 addresses functional safety for road vehicles. It concerns
hazards caused by malfunctioning behavior of electrical and electronic
systems. Functional safety and cybersecurity are related but distinct
disciplines.

3. Cybersecurity Risk
Cybersecurity risk assessment considers potential threats, vulnerabilities,
attack paths, impacts and suitable mitigations. Conclusions should be based
on the system context and documented evidence.

4. Functional Safety
Functional safety activities analyze hazards and risks arising from
malfunctions. A suspicious network message alone is not enough to determine
a safety classification or confirm a hazardous vehicle behavior.

5. Relationship Between Safety and Security
A cyberattack may cause or contribute to unsafe behavior, but not every
cybersecurity event creates a safety hazard. Security and safety teams may
need to coordinate their investigations.

6. Evidence and Compliance
A prototype detector or an AI-generated report does not establish compliance
with an automotive standard. Compliance requires appropriate engineering
processes, evidence, reviews and validation.

7. Applying These Concepts to a CAN IDS
- Record the observed event and its technical evidence.
- Separate cybersecurity indicators from confirmed impacts.
- Document assumptions, uncertainties and possible consequences.
- Recommend further analysis and validation where necessary.
- Avoid claiming certification or standards compliance without evidence.

Source note:
Educational summary only. Consult the applicable official editions of
ISO/SAE 21434 and ISO 26262 for authoritative requirements. This document
does not reproduce the standards or establish compliance.
